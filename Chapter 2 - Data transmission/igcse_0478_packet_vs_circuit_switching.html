What changed?

Packet switching

Packets are forwarded through routers. Alternative routes may be available if a link becomes busy or fails.

Circuit switching

A dedicated route is reserved for the duration of the connection. If a link on that route fails, the connection may be interrupted and need to be re-established.

Advantages and disadvantages

Packet switching

  • Advantage: Network links can be shared by packets from many users, making efficient use of capacity.
  • Advantage: Packets may be rerouted if an alternative route is available.
  • Disadvantage: Packets can be delayed and may arrive out of order.

Circuit switching

  • Advantage: Once established, the dedicated path provides predictable use of the reserved capacity.
  • Disadvantage: Reserved capacity may be wasted when no data is being sent.
  • Disadvantage: A failure on the dedicated path can interrupt the connection.
IGCSE reminder: Do not say packet switching always avoids congestion or that every packet takes a different route. Routers make forwarding decisions, and packets may still be delayed or lost.
