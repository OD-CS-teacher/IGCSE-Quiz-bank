IGCSE 0478 — key ideas

  • Packet: a small part of a larger message.
  • Header: control information used to handle and route a packet.
  • Payload: the actual data being transmitted.
  • Sequence number: helps identify packet order and supports reassembly.
  • Error checking: helps detect data corruption.
  • Packet switching: packets are forwarded towards their destination; they may take different routes and arrive out of order.
Exam note: Real packet fields depend on the protocol. Source and destination addresses, sequencing and error-checking information may be carried in different protocol headers or other parts of the communication process. Not every packet contains all these fields in the same format.
